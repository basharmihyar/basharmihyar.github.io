Privacy

Bashar OS · last updated 19 October 2026

Bashar OS is a personal productivity app and a set of helpers ("the crew") that Bashar Mihyar runs for himself. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What it reads

  • Gmail: message headers, snippets and text, to spot emails that need a reply, receipts and bills, and bookings with a time and place.
  • Google Calendar: events, read-only, to plan the day and drives.

What it changes

Only Gmail labels. It may add or remove labels such as "BOS/Needs reply" and "BOS/Newsletters", and archive old newsletters by removing the Inbox label. It never sends, replies to, forwards, drafts, trashes or deletes email, and it never changes calendar events.

Where the data goes

Summaries (counts, reminders and short notes) are written to the owner's own private notes folder on his own Google Drive and iCloud Drive. The helpers run on the owner's own computer and use AI models from Google (Gemini) to read and summarise; nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train models by Bashar OS. Card, bank account and ID numbers are never stored.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Bashar OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account › Third-party connections. Stored summaries can be deleted from the owner's notes folder.
